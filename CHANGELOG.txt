Aura-CTRL - Changelog
================================================================
All notable changes to Aura-CTRL are listed here, newest first.
Versions follow semantic versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH):
  MAJOR - incompatible changes (settings, file layout, hooks)
  MINOR - new features, backward compatible
  PATCH - bug fixes


[1.0.0] - 2026-11-08
----------------------------------------------------------------
First release. Tested on ASUS ROG Rapture GT-AXE11000 with
Asuswrt-Merlin 3004.388.12_2, together with scribe, Skynet, scMerlin
and ZW-DFS. Released under GPL-3.0-or-later. Author: iggenio.
Developed with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic).

WebUI page
- Administration > Aura-CTRL, in English: Aura RGB switch, status card
  (what the logo shows, active alerts with details, Acknowledge) and
  three tabs: Lighting, Alerts, Diagnostics.
- Settings saved in the background, without the progress page; one
  notice at the bottom of the window for every message.

Lighting
- One scene, or separate day and night scenes with start times.
- 24 animations: the 14 effects of the light controller (including
  the hidden Pulse, Siren and Siren multicolor) and 10 made by
  Aura-CTRL: Multi-colour, Colour steps, Traffic meter, Colour fade,
  Colour breathing, Alternate, Two-colour siren, Heartbeat, Candle,
  Sweep. Editor with colours (1-3), speed and direction; previews
  follow speed and direction.
- Two-colour siren: fast flashes like an emergency vehicle; the light
  zones form two sets that flash in turn, one in each colour.
- Traffic meter: bar meter with three zones always on, each from 20%
  to 100% across its third of the traffic; colours of your choice
  (default green, yellow, red); 100% is the line speed entered by the
  user (download and upload in Mbps, from a speed test); measured
  every 3, 5 or 10 seconds.
- Brightness (day and night), with a curve that matches the eye.
- Smooth transitions (fade through black, no flash of the previous
  colour) and Eco mode (no High CPU animations).

Alerts
- 11 alerts with fixed rules and severities: Router tampering, Evil
  twin detected, Internet down, Protection stalled (Skynet), Unknown
  device, Radar detected and Radar check in progress (ZW-DFS), High
  temperature, USB missing or almost full, Firmware update available,
  Admin session active.
- Family colours with Custom 1 and Custom 2 (renamable); per alert:
  colour source, animation, duration, shown at night, Test.
- Several alerts at once: reminders every 10 seconds; with a known
  zone map up to 3 non-critical alerts share the logo.

Diagnostics
- Light zone test, one button per zone (1 to 5, 3-10 s); on models
  without a verified map the user chooses 3 or 5 zones.
- Colour correction (red, green, blue), applied on the logo only, with
  test colours and a run through the family colours; GT-AXE11000
  default 100/60/57 (measured).
- Router model, CPU temperature, unknown devices, evil twin BSSIDs,
  recent events.

Script
- Light on the router: settings read once per run, nvram variables
  with "nvram get" and a 30-minute cache for the ones that rarely
  change, parsing with shell built-ins (about 30 processes and half a
  second per check on a GT-AXE11000).
- Safe with the 32-bit arithmetic of the router shell.
- Commands: install, startup, check, event, wanevent, ack, test,
  identify, colours, calib, effect, status, version, uninstall.
- Never runs "nvram commit"; uninstall restores the original Aura
  values.

Known limitations
- High temperature uses a fixed 90 °C limit.
- Firmware update available relies on webs_state_flag.
- Skynet update detection reads Skynet events.log.
- The previews of the router effects are approximations; reports from
  users of other models are welcome.
